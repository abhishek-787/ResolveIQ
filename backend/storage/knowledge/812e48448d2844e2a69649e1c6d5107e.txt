SHOPEASE
Damaged & Defective Items Policy

Policy ID: SE-CS-002
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 01 September 2026
Policy Owner: Customer Experience Operations
Applies To: Orders fulfilled directly by ShopEase

1. Purpose

This policy defines the process for resolving customer claims involving products that arrive physically damaged, defective on arrival, incomplete, or materially different from their expected working condition.

The purpose of this policy is to provide customers with a fair and timely resolution while ensuring that damage and defect claims are supported by sufficient order and product evidence.

2. Scope

This policy applies to products purchased directly through ShopEase and fulfilled by ShopEase or an approved ShopEase fulfillment partner.

Marketplace items fulfilled entirely by an independent seller may be subject to additional seller-specific terms.

3. Damaged on Arrival

A product is considered damaged on arrival when physical damage is present at the time the customer first receives or opens the shipment.

Examples include:

- cracked displays or glass;
- broken housings or frames;
- major dents;
- damaged ports or connectors;
- broken internal components;
- liquid leakage from an appliance;
- severe packaging damage that has also damaged the product.

Customers should report visible physical damage within 7 calendar days of the recorded delivery date.

4. Defective on Arrival

A product may be considered defective on arrival when it does not operate according to its normal intended function despite reasonable setup and troubleshooting.

Examples include:

- product does not power on;
- battery fails to charge;
- display remains non-functional;
- device cannot complete required initial setup;
- essential hardware function is unavailable.

A reported technical problem does not automatically confirm that a product is defective. ShopEase may first require standard troubleshooting steps from the relevant product manual.

5. Evidence Requirements

ShopEase may request evidence before approving a damaged or defective item resolution.

Evidence may include:

- ShopEase order number;
- clear photograph of the damaged product;
- photograph of the outer shipping package;
- photograph of the shipping label;
- photograph showing the product serial number;
- screenshot or photograph of an error;
- description of troubleshooting already attempted.

For electronics priced above INR 15,000, photographic evidence of visible physical damage should normally be collected before a replacement or refund is approved.

If the customer is unable to provide evidence because of accessibility or technical limitations, the case may be escalated for manual review.

6. Resolution Options

When a claim is validated, ShopEase may offer one of the following resolutions:

- replacement with the same product;
- replacement with an equivalent product when the original item is unavailable;
- refund to the original payment method;
- repair or manufacturer warranty support when appropriate.

For damage reported within 7 calendar days of delivery, replacement is the preferred first resolution when the same product is available.

If a replacement is unavailable, a refund may be offered.

A customer may request a refund instead of replacement, but high-value refund requests may require additional approval.

7. Wrong Product or Missing Components

If the customer received a different product from the item shown in the ShopEase order record, the issue should be handled as an incorrect-item fulfillment case.

The customer may be requested to provide a photograph showing:

- the received product;
- product packaging;
- shipping label;
- model or SKU information where visible.

Confirmed incorrect-item cases are eligible for replacement or refund without a restocking fee.

If only a component or accessory is missing, ShopEase may ship the missing component instead of replacing the complete product.

8. Exclusions

This policy does not normally cover damage caused after successful delivery by:

- accidental drops;
- customer misuse;
- unauthorized modification;
- improper electrical supply;
- liquid exposure not attributable to shipping;
- normal wear and tear.

Claims showing evidence of customer-caused damage should be escalated when the cause cannot be confidently determined.

9. High-Value Items

For the purpose of support resolution, a high-value item is an item with an order-item value greater than INR 15,000.

Refunds for high-value items require human support approval before execution.

Replacement recommendations may be prepared automatically, but the final replacement action may also require approval according to current ShopEase support controls.

10. Escalation

A case should be escalated when:

- evidence is insufficient or contradictory;
- the customer disputes a previous damage decision;
- fraud or repeated abnormal claim activity is suspected;
- the item value exceeds normal automated support limits;
- the policy does not clearly support a resolution;
- the requested action falls outside standard replacement or refund rules.

11. Resolution Communication

The customer should be informed of:

- the decision;
- reason for the decision;
- next required action;
- expected replacement or refund timeline;
- any return-shipping requirement.

Support responses must not promise a refund, replacement, or delivery date unless the relevant ShopEase system confirms that action.

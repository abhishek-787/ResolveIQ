SHOPEASE
Returns & Refunds Policy

Policy ID: SE-CS-001
Version: 3.0
Effective Date: 01 September 2026
Policy Owner: Customer Experience Operations
Applies To: Eligible purchases made through ShopEase

1. Purpose

This policy defines standard eligibility, timelines, conditions, and resolution procedures for customer returns and refunds.

Product-specific warranty terms and the Damaged & Defective Items Policy may override the general conditions in this document when applicable.

2. Standard Return Window

Most eligible products may be requested for return within 15 calendar days of the recorded delivery date.

The return request must be initiated through ShopEase customer support or the ShopEase return process before the end of the applicable return window.

3. Condition for Standard Returns

For a standard return unrelated to product damage or defect, the product should normally:

- include original accessories;
- include supplied manuals and components;
- not show significant customer-caused damage;
- not have been materially altered;
- be returned in reasonable condition.

Opened packaging alone does not automatically make an otherwise eligible item non-returnable.

4. Electronics

Electronics may be returned within the applicable return period when the product satisfies return eligibility requirements.

When the reported reason involves technical malfunction, ShopEase may require troubleshooting before deciding whether the case should be treated as a return, replacement, or warranty claim.

Physical damage present at delivery must be handled according to the ShopEase Damaged & Defective Items Policy, Policy SE-CS-002.

5. Incorrect Items

A customer who receives a product different from the product listed in their ShopEase order is eligible for corrective resolution.

Depending on stock availability and customer preference, ShopEase may provide:

- replacement with the ordered product; or
- refund to the original payment method.

ShopEase may request photographic evidence of the received item and its packaging.

6. Non-Returnable Conditions

A return may be rejected when:

- the return period has expired;
- there is clear evidence of customer-caused damage;
- required product components are missing due to customer loss;
- the item was materially modified;
- the item belongs to a product category explicitly marked final sale.

A case should be escalated when eligibility cannot be determined confidently from available information.

7. Refund Method

Approved refunds should normally be issued to the original payment method.

Where the original payment method cannot receive a refund, customer support may escalate the case for an alternative approved refund method.

Store credit must not be substituted for a monetary refund without customer agreement.

8. Refund Processing Time

After ShopEase confirms that a refund is approved, internal refund initiation should normally occur within 2 business days.

Bank, card network, or payment-provider processing may require an additional 3 to 7 business days.

The customer must not be promised an exact bank-credit date unless that date is confirmed by the payment provider.

9. Return Shipping

When ShopEase is responsible for the issue, including confirmed damaged, defective, or incorrect items, ShopEase should normally provide return-shipping instructions without charging the customer a return fee.

For standard preference-based returns, return-shipping conditions may depend on the product category and current ShopEase terms.

10. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be considered when:

- a minor accessory is missing;
- cosmetic damage does not materially affect operation;
- customer and ShopEase mutually agree to keep the product.

Partial refund amounts require human approval before execution.

11. Repeated or Unusual Claims

Repeated returns, conflicting evidence, or unusual claim patterns may be referred for manual review.

A manual review does not automatically mean that the customer's request will be denied.

12. Policy Conflicts

If this general policy conflicts with a more specific ShopEase policy, the more specific policy should be used for the relevant issue.

Examples:

- physical delivery damage → Damaged & Defective Items Policy;
- shipping loss → Shipping & Delivery Policy;
- manufacturer warranty issue → Warranty Policy.

13. Customer Communication

Every return or refund response should clearly state:

- whether the request is eligible;
- the policy basis for the decision;
- any evidence or action still required;
- the next processing step;
- expected processing time when known.
